# 🤖 Autonomous AI Research & Task Agent

An advanced AI-powered research assistant built with **Python, Streamlit, Groq, RAG, SQLite, PDF processing, and autonomous AI workflows**.

The system accepts a research objective, creates a research plan, performs web research, retrieves relevant information from uploaded PDF documents, evaluates the findings, and generates a professional research report.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The Autonomous AI Research & Task Agent is designed to perform multi-step research automatically.

```text
User Research Objective
        ↓
AI Research Planner
        ↓
Autonomous Research
        ↓
Web Research + PDF Documents
        ↓
Research Evaluation
        ↓
AI Report Generation
        ↓
PDF Report
        ↓
SQLite Research History
